====================================================================
THE PLAN: WHAT'S BEING BUILT NEXT
====================================================================
   Each part is built and tested as its own plugin first. Once they all
   work, they're combined into one channel strip.


====================================================================
THE CHANNEL STRIP, IN SIGNAL ORDER
====================================================================
   1. INPUT         Trim, and a polarity (phase) flip for multi-mic drums.

   2. GATE          For toms, and for kick and snare bleed. It filters
                    what it listens to (like Easy Comp's IGNORE BELOW),
                    so a kick in the snare mic doesn't open the snare's
                    gate.

   3. ANALOG VIBE   The "preamp" knob. 0 = clean (switched off, costs
                    nothing), middle = analog warmth, full = saturation:
                    a beefy rough edge on rock bass, a growl on synths.
                    Level-matched, so it changes the tone, not the
                    volume. Keeps the lowest notes clean at high
                    settings. (Heavier amp-style distortion for guitars
                    and leads will be a separate plugin.)

   4. EQ            High- and low-pass filters, low and high shelves,
                    and two bell bands. Two modes:
                      CHANNEL           normal EQ (the default)
                      BUS & MASTERING   linear phase: keeps the timing
                                        of the sound intact, adds a short
                                        delay, can soften drum hits
                    BUS & MASTERING gets a precision setting: lower means
                    less delay and less smear before hits.

   5. DE-ESSER      Adjustable frequency range, and it turns down only the
                    harsh band, not the whole track. LISTEN button to hear
                    what it's catching. Presets for vocals, harsh cymbals
                    and bright hi-hats.

   6. COMPRESSOR    Easy Comp, plus:
                      CHARACTER   our own take on feedback compression,
                                  in the zone of the API 2500's OLD mode
                                  (not a copy)
                      a THRUST-style option for IGNORE BELOW

   7. TRANSIENT     Easy Transient.

   8. OUTPUT        Level, and a hard clipper: a ceiling control, an
                    optional softness knob (0 = hard), and a light that
                    shows how much it's shaving off. If it's regularly
                    taking more than 2-3 dB, turn the track down earlier
                    in the chain.

   Occasional extras:
   - EQ order switch: the EQ after the compressor.
   - Sidechain input, so the kick can duck the bass.


====================================================================
RULES FOR EVERYTHING
====================================================================
   - Anything switched off costs no processor time.
   - Oversampling only for the ANALOG VIBE and the clipper: Off while
     mixing, 4x or 8x when exporting (it switches by itself in DAWs that
     say they're exporting), plus a manual switch for fine-tuning.
   - The strip keeps the same small delay at every oversampling setting,
     so drum mics never shift against each other.
   - No oversampling for the compressor (CHARACTER included) unless real
     projects show it's needed.
   - Not copies of existing products: our own sound, in the same zone
     where that's the aim.
