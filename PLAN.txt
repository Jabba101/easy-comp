====================================================================
THE PLAN: WHAT'S BEING BUILT NEXT
====================================================================
   The suite is called Aardvark Easy Suite: that's the folder your DAW
   lists the plugins in. Each part is built and tested as its own
   plugin first. Once they all work, they're combined into one
   channel strip: the Aardvark Easy Strip.


====================================================================
THE CHANNEL STRIP, IN SIGNAL ORDER
====================================================================
   1. INPUT         Trim, and a polarity (phase) flip for multi-mic drums.

   2. GATE          Easy Gate (done). For toms, and for kick and snare
                    bleed. IGNORE BELOW and IGNORE ABOVE filter what it
                    listens to, so a kick in the snare mic doesn't open
                    the snare's gate.

   3. ANALOG VIBE   Easy Amp. The "preamp" knob: 0 = clean (switched
                    off, costs nothing), middle = analog warmth, full =
                    saturation: a beefy rough edge on rock bass, a growl
                    on synths. Level-matched, so it changes the tone,
                    not the volume. Keeps the lowest notes clean at high
                    settings. (Heavier amp-style distortion for guitars
                    and leads will be a separate plugin.)

   4. EQ            Easy EQ. High- and low-pass filters, low and high
                    shelves, and two bell bands, with the curve drawn so
                    you can see and drag it. Mid-side: each band can work
                    on STEREO, MID or SIDE, plus a MONO BASS knob. The bells get narrower the
                    harder you boost or cut, which is part of what makes
                    API's EQs sound musical (our own take, not a copy).
                    Two modes:
                      CHANNEL           normal EQ (the default)
                      BUS & MASTERING   linear phase: keeps the timing
                                        of the sound intact, adds a short
                                        delay, can soften drum hits
                    BUS & MASTERING gets a precision setting: lower means
                    less delay and less smear before hits.

   5. DE-ESSER      Easy DS (done). FROM and TO set the harsh band, FIND
                    sets them for you, and it turns down just the harsh
                    band or (TURNS DOWN: WHOLE SOUND) everything, the
                    classic style. LISTEN to hear what it's catching.

   6. COMPRESSOR    Easy Comp (done), with CHARACTER: STYLE MODERN or
                    VINTAGE (our own take on feedback compression, in the
                    zone of the API 2500's OLD mode, not a copy) and LOW
                    END: THRUST.

   7. TRANSIENT     Easy Trans.

   8. GATED REVERB  Bitch-Slap (done as its own plugin, 1.0.0), right on
                    the track: punch in a big gated reverb without
                    hunting for a reverb plugin. MIX, LENGTH (how long it
                    rings before it slams shut) and SYNC (LENGTH as a
                    note at the song's tempo). Switched off, it costs
                    nothing. Its own plugin stays, for using it as a send
                    on a bus.

   9. OUTPUT        Level, and a LUFS meter: the tall output meter
                    switches between PEAK dB and LUFS (loudness as ears
                    hear it, what streaming services use) with one small
                    pushbutton; a drum counter shows the song-so-far
                    (integrated) LUFS, click it to reset; the ticker
                    explains it and the usual targets (e.g. around -14
                    LUFS for most streaming). Same K-weighted maths as
                    AUTO-GAIN, so it costs almost nothing. Most useful on
                    buses and the master.
                    And Easy Clip: first of all a brick-wall
                    hard clipper. Two modes (the hidden settings keep
                    working - switching only shows or hides knobs):
                      SIMPLE    PUSH (drive into it), CEILING, SOFTNESS
                                (0 = brick wall, up = rounder), PROTECT
                                LOWS (below a chosen frequency the low end
                                isn't clipped: kick and bass stay clean)
                      ADVANCED  adds the sound-design knobs: TOP and
                                BOTTOM (each half of the wave clipped
                                differently, linkable), BIAS, BLEND
                                (parallel clipping)
                    A lamp on ADVANCED stays lit while any advanced
                    setting isn't neutral, so nothing hidden surprises
                    you. On the strip the Clip slab flips in place
                    between the two (CEILING never moves). A readout
                    shows how much it's shaving off; if it's regularly
                    more than 2-3 dB, turn the track down earlier in
                    the chain.

   Occasional extras:
   - Changing the order: the panels stay where they are, and a small
     "signal flow" row along the top (GATE -> DRIVE -> EQ -> ...) shows
     the order the sound goes through. To start with, a few named
     orders or swap switches rather than free dragging: e.g. "EQ after
     the compressor", "De-ess before drive". Presets can set the order
     too. Changing it while playing crossfades (no clicks), and the
     strip's small delay stays the same whatever the order (Easy Gate
     and Easy DS share one 2 ms look-ahead).
   - Sidechain input, so the kick can duck the bass (done in Easy Gate
     and Easy Comp; the strip will share one sidechain input).

   Strip presets to include:
   - A couple of lo-fi presets: a broad de-ess (TURNS DOWN: WHOLE
     SOUND) together with overdriven saturation from Easy Amp. Try the
     de-esser both before and after the drive: before stops S sounds
     distorting into a spitty hiss; after tames the fizz the drive
     adds.


====================================================================
RULES FOR EVERYTHING
====================================================================
   - Anything switched off costs no processor time.
   - Oversampling only for Easy Amp and Easy Clip: Off while mixing, 4x
     or 8x when exporting (it switches by itself in DAWs that say
     they're exporting), plus a manual switch for fine-tuning.
     (Easy Clip on its own: AUTO is 4x while mixing, 8x when exporting -
     a lone clipper is cheap at 4x and OFF sounds noticeably harsher.
     On the strip, with one on every track, AUTO may need to be OFF
     while mixing to save power: decide when the strip is built.)
   - The strip keeps the same small delay at every oversampling setting,
     so drum mics never shift against each other.
   - No oversampling for the compressor (CHARACTER included) unless real
     projects show it's needed.
   - Not copies of existing products: our own sound, in the same zone
     where that's the aim.

BUILD ORDER
====================================================================
   1. Easy Gate            done (1.0.0)
   2. Easy DS              done (1.0.0)
   3. Easy Comp update     done (1.6.0)
   4. Bitch-Slap           done (1.0.0): the gated reverb, its own plugin
                           (on the track or as a send); joins the strip later
   5. Easy EQ              done (1.0.0, all three stages at once); as decided (2026-10-29):
                           LOW 30-400 Hz, LOW MID 75 Hz-1.2 kHz, HIGH MID
                           800 Hz-8 kHz, HIGH 2.5-20 kHz (LOW / HIGH: BELL or
                           SHELF); gain +-12 dB; proportional Q (about 1.5
                           octaves wide at +-2 dB, a third of an octave at
                           +-12 dB, like a 550); separate frequency and gain
                           knobs, drag a point on the screen for both;
                           HPF 20-500 Hz and LPF 2-20 kHz, OFF at their
                           ends, 12 or 24 dB/oct; filters accurate right up
                           to 20 kHz (no cramping, no oversampling);
                           ST / MID / SIDE per band, MONO BASS 20-300 Hz
                           (OFF at the bottom, dims on mono); MASTER = linear
                           phase, the delay changes only when it's pressed;
                           a faint spectrum analyser behind the curve, with
                           an off switch; band LISTEN (hear only what a band
                           covers); AUTO-GAIN off by default (how the level
                           moves with the EQ is part of the art); no STEPPED
                           knobs; the frequency guide with the VOCALS row.
                           Build in stages: bands + filters + curve, then
                           mid-side, then MASTER.
   6. Easy Clip            done (1.0.1): SIMPLE / ADVANCED, PROTECT LOWS,
                           oversampling AUTO (4x mixing, 8x export) / OFF /
                           4x / 8x, a look-ahead safety net at the CEILING
      Easy Amp             done (1.0.0): DRIVE (clean -> valve warmth ->
                           overdriven growl, AC/DC-guitar and Motorhead-bass
                           ballpark), TONE, TIGHT, MIX; AUTO-GAIN on by
                           default; same oversampling as Easy Clip
   7. The channel strip    the Aardvark Easy Strip. Stage 1 done (0.1.0):
                           the sound and plumbing in a plain test layout -
                           each module identical to its plugin (tested
                           sample for sample, alone and all eight in a
                           row), switched-off modules skipped, settings
                           both ways (DAW <-> module windows), shared
                           sidechain and OVERSAMPLING, INPUT TRIM / polarity,
                           OUTPUT LEVEL, meters, LUFS S / I, whole-strip
                           INIT and BYPASS, saving.
                           Stage 2 done (0.2.0): the HOLOGRAM skin (every
                           module's controls on one panel, the big screen
                           with a page per module, the ticker with the
                           standalones' own hints, per-module INIT and Pr,
                           DIM), SETUP (size 60-150% or drag, skin
                           HOLOGRAM / CLASSIC, ticker, meter 0 = 0 dBFS or
                           -18 dBFS), resizable window.
                           Stage 3 done (0.3.0): ~80 strip presets built from
                           the modules' own presets, A / B for the strip and
                           for Comp (Easy Comp 1.7.0 too), EQ MASTER
                           PRECISION (Easy EQ 1.2.0 too), DISPLAY FOCUS row,
                           DISPLAY BRIGHTNESS in SETUP.
                           0.4.0: AUTO GAIN for the whole strip (above the
                           OUTPUT meter; only turns down while CLIP is on).
                           GATE + DS sharing one 2 ms look-ahead: looked
                           into and parked. Its purpose (the strip's delay
                           never changing, whatever the module order) is
                           already met: every module's delay always counts,
                           on or off. Truly sharing it would save 2 ms
                           (of ~7.7 ms, which Samplitude lines up anyway)
                           but means either the DS hearing something
                           different from Easy DS, or running AMP and the
                           EQ twice. Revisit only if a low-latency mode for
                           recording through the strip is ever wanted.
                           Later: module order, Comp mid-side, WEAPON
                           SELECT (Easy SFX).

LATER
====================================================================
   - The optional Cold War look: choose a direction from the mockups
     (A, B, C or a mix). The current clean look stays too.
   - A resizable window.
   - A / B: in Easy EQ, Easy Comp and the strip (done).
   - Easy Comp: mid-side - one WORKS ON: STEREO / MID / SIDE choice
     (default STEREO, so old presets sound the same), e.g. squash the
     middle of a drum bus while the cymbals and room breathe. In the
     standalone first, then the strip. (The full two-compressor M/S
     version is mastering territory: not planned.)
   - Once the essentials are perfected: EASY SFX, an effects rack.
     WEAPON SELECT swaps the whole strip layout for the rack; closing the
     rack brings the strip back. The brainstorm (30 October 2026):
       - FLANGER / CHORUS with a vibrato mode, to make things feel
         wider; rates lockable to the song's tempo
       - MOD FILTER: tempo-locked pulses, wide stereo filtering (left
         and right moving apart)
       - STEREO DELAY: tempo-lockable (slapback on vocals and more)
       - AUTO-WAH: an automatic wah pedal (follows how hard you play)
       - side-to-side TREMOLO (auto-pan): confirmed, its own effect;
         tempo-lockable like the others
       - a TRANSPARENT LIMITER - ONLY if it matches the transparency of IK
         Multimedia's Stealth Limiter, which the user always uses. The
         bar: compared blind and level-matched on the user's own mixes,
         it must be indistinguishable or better; if not, it isn't
         shipped and Stealth Limiter stays. (Likely ingredients: look-
         ahead, true-peak oversampling, programme-dependent release,
         gain changes spread so they don't distort the lows.)

DONE
====================================================================
   - Bitch-Slap 1.0.1: four Mix Bus presets (Subtle Size, Rock & Punk
     Punch, 80s Space, Synced 1/16). On a whole mix the reverb comes out
     far louder than on one drum, so their MIX (11-19%) was set by
     measuring the reverb on real mixes.
   - Bitch-Slap 1.0.0: the gated reverb - the "burst" kind (the 80s
     digital way): every sound, ghost notes and bleed included, gets
     its own burst of wide reverb that stops dead. MIX (up to 50% it
     only adds reverb; 100% = reverb only, for a send), LENGTH 50-1000
     ms, SYNC: TEMPO (LENGTH as a note, 1/32 to 1/2, following the
     song's tempo), BYPASS. A display of your hits and their bursts. 22
     presets: snare, toms and kick, whole kit, electronic, vocals, FX
     send, tempo sync - for rock, punk, synthwave, darksynth and jazz.
     Moving LENGTH never clicks: each hit keeps the burst it started
     with. No added delay; about half a percent of one core with
     1-second bursts, nothing in silence, bypassed or at MIX 0%.
   - Easy Comp 1.6.0: CHARACTER - STYLE: MODERN or VINTAGE, and LOW END:
     NORMAL or THRUST - and a sidechain input (LISTENS TO). VINTAGE
     follows loud passages closely and eases back gently when the sound
     drops away; the same peak amount as MODERN, and less distortion on
     low notes. THRUST: the detector hears 3 dB less per octave below
     about 2 kHz. 9 new presets (VINTAGE / THRUST versions, and "Sidechain
     Tricks"). MODERN sounds exactly as before, and old projects open on
     MODERN, NORMAL and THIS TRACK.
   - Easy DS 1.0.0: FROM, TO, FIND, THRESHOLD, RANGE, TURNS DOWN (HARSH
     BAND or WHOLE SOUND), LISTEN, and a 2 ms look-ahead. THRESHOLD is
     compared with the rest of the sound (and, half as much, how loud
     it's been lately), so it works whether the singer whispers or
     belts, and still catches a loud crash cymbal over the kick. 14
     presets, tuned on real recorded speech (16 speakers), sung vocals
     with real S sounds, and a real drum kit. Uses about the same
     processor time as Easy Comp.
   - Easy Gate 1.0.0: THRESHOLD, RANGE, ATTACK, HOLD, RELEASE, IGNORE
     BELOW / ABOVE, a sidechain input, LISTEN, and a 2 ms look-ahead so
     the start of every hit gets through. 17 presets. Uses about half
     the processor time of Easy Comp.
   - The new names: the maker is now Aardvark Easy Suite (it was Home
     Studio), and Easy Transient is now Easy Trans (1.2.1). Projects
     and your own presets keep working.
   - Easy Comp 1.5.0 and Easy Transient 1.2.0: the faster engine, about a
     third of the processor time while playing, and almost none on silent
     tracks. The sound is unchanged.
