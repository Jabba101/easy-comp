WHERE THE TEST AUDIO COMES FROM
===============================
The test material (about 1 GB of WAVs) isn't kept in the repository: it's
rebuilt from free sample collections on GitHub, then arranged into
realistic tracks by the scripts in ../PresetTuning (make_material.py,
gate_material.py, ds_material.py). setup_workspace.sh does all of it.

Each collection is a partial ("sparse") checkout of a public repository,
at the exact commit used, or a list of single files. Check each project's
own licence before using the audio for anything else.

  /home/claude/samples/librosa-data         https://github.com/librosa/data
      commit 38f4b06556fa0ff1acda5e677d8ba05d1bc0fff0
      files  /audio/*.hq.ogg  /index.json  /audio/snare-accelerate.ogg

  /home/claude/samples/tonejs-audio         https://github.com/Tonejs/audio
      commit efd8296360f9526e379bfbe5c1698ff54d6a1d34
      files  /drum-samples/acoustic-kit/*  /drum-samples/Kit3/*  /drum-samples/Kit8/*
             /drum-samples/breakbeat8/*  /drum-samples/breakbeat9/*  /drum-samples/breakbeat13/*
             /drum-samples/loops/*  /drum-samples/*.mp3  /loop/*.mp3

             guitar-nylon, piano, saxophone, trumpet, trombone, french-horn, violin, cello,

  /home/claude/samples/audiomnist/wav       AudioMNIST (spoken digits, 16+ speakers)
      https://github.com/soerenab/AudioMNIST - the 960 files in audiomnist_urls.txt,
      downloaded flat into wav/; audiomnist_meta.json is the speakers list
      (data/audioMNIST_meta.txt in that project)

  /home/claude/samples/virtuosity/flac      Virtuosity Drums (a real acoustic kit: overheads,
                                            room, and the kick and snare mics)
      https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums - the files in
      virtuosity_urls.txt, downloaded flat into flac/

The tools also need ffmpeg (to decode mp3/ogg/flac) and Python 3 with
numpy, scipy and matplotlib.
