# Aardvark Easy Suite

Beginner-friendly mixing plugins (VST3) for Windows, built with [JUCE](https://juce.com):

- **Easy Comp** – compressor
- **Easy Trans** – transient shaper
- **Easy Gate** – gate
- **Easy DS** – de-esser
- **Easy Verb** – live rooms, clubs and halls, a plate, and the Bitch-Slap 80s gated burst (formerly Bitch-Slap)
- **Easy Clip** – clipper
- **Easy Amp** – valve amp, from warmth to an overdriven growl
- **Easy EQ** – four-band EQ with filters, mid-side and linear phase
- **Easy Tape** – a real tape machine: 15 ips, 30 ips, or cassette for lo-fi
- **Easy Delay** – a stereo delay, in time with your song
- **Easy Tremolo** – the volume pulsing, or side to side, locked to the beat
- **Easy Wah** – an automatic wah: follows your playing, or rocks in time
- **Easy Flange** – flanger, chorus and vibrato
- **Easy Limiter** – a clean, transparent true-peak ceiling
- **Easy Filter** – a moving filter: sweeps, wobbles and tempo-locked pulses, wide stereo
- **Easy Master** – the mastering chain for the master bus (resonance tamer, mastering EQ, multiband, compressor, punch and width – each with separate mid / side controls – into a true-peak limiter, with LUFS meters, and a REFERENCE track - a finished song to A / B against at matched loudness)
- **Easy Unbleed** – cleans the other instruments' ghost out of separated stems (UVR5 and the like)
- **Easy Strip** – all of them in one channel strip, with LEARN (automatic gain staging), THE MIX (every strip in the project: LEARN ALL and a rough BALANCE by role and style) and DESK (classic console colour, valve to Helios)

Every knob explains itself in a help bar, and every plugin comes with presets.

**Getting the plugins:** the Actions tab builds them on every change; download the newest
run's artifact. See [HOW TO BUILD.txt](HOW%20TO%20BUILD.txt) for the step-by-step guide.
